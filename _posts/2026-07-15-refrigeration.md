Last summer as I was finishing my build out my checking account was running low so I had to get a cheap fridge. 
That worked well enough until a couple weeks ago. This morning I had someone out to take a look and learned that
I've got a freon leak and replacing the compressor would cost more than the price of the fridge. So, it's time
for an upgrade! Of course I still have a low balance in the checking account so I'll have to borrow money for the
first time. But lesson learned: sometimes saving money ends up costing more money in the end. 
And if you're looking at fridges from Webstaurant Store, don't bother with the Avantco brand!
